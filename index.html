A showcase of my recent work in digital design

I am a current undergraduate student studying Digital Media at the University of Leeds. This portfolio showcases a combination of work produced throughout my course, with a primary focus on Interface Design, which is a module that combines UI/UX design principles with front-end development to create responsive, accessible, and visually engaging digital products. My work prioritises usability, performance, and clean, well-structured code.
